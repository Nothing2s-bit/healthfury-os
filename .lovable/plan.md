# Perfect the Emergency Button

## Goal
Turn the current Emergency tile into a dedicated, dependable emergency screen for South African users instead of opening the health scanner.

## What will change
- Link the dashboard Emergency button to a new `/emergency` screen.
- Add clear one-tap actions for **112**, **10177 ambulance**, and **10111 police**, with confirmation to prevent accidental calls.
- Add a prominent SOS control that can capture the user’s location, show location accuracy, and let them copy or share an emergency message.
- Provide fast links to nearby emergency facilities using the phone’s maps app and to HealthFury messages for notifying care staff.
- Include concise first-response guidance for major emergencies while clearly stating that the screen does not replace emergency services.
- Keep the existing Egyptian gold design, but use a strong red emergency treatment with accessible controls and reduced-motion support.

## Safety and reliability
- Keep emergency calling available even when location permission is denied.
- Explain unsupported or failed location/share actions in plain language.
- Never claim an ambulance was dispatched; the user must complete the phone call.
- Avoid storing or broadcasting precise location without a deliberate user action.

## Validation
- Check the new route on the current mobile size and desktop.
- Verify call links, location permission handling, copy/share actions, navigation, metadata, and absence of browser errors.
